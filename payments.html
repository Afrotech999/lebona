How it works

Manual, and deliberately so

Handling payment by hand keeps our costs, and therefore your fees, low while we build. It does mean a couple of extra steps.

1

You get matched first

Nothing is charged during the intake form. Payment only comes up once a coordinator has proposed a specific therapist and you have said yes to them.

2

Your coordinator sends the details

The exact amount, the merchant number for your country, and a reference. This comes through the channel you chose, WhatsApp, Telegram, phone or email.

3

You pay and send proof

Send a screenshot or the transaction ID back on the same channel. This is how we verify it. There is no automatic reconciliation yet, so the proof matters.

4

We confirm and book

Once verified, your session is confirmed in writing with the date, time and how to join. Usually the same working day.

Protect yourself from fraud

  • · Lebona will never ask for payment before you have been matched to a named therapist.
  • · We will never ask for your mobile money PIN, or ask you to reverse a transaction.
  • · Only send money to a number your coordinator gave you on the channel you registered.
  • · If anything feels off, stop and check with us first.
