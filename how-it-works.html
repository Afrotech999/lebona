How it works

From the intake form to the first session.

Matching at Lebona is done by people, not an algorithm. That makes it better and slower. Here is exactly what happens, how long each part takes, who reads your answers, and what you can change.

Start the intake form
01

Complete the intake form

About 8 minutes · free · no account

One question per screen, so nothing feels like paperwork. You'll be asked which country you're in, what kind of support you want, which language you're most comfortable in, and a short well-being check adapted from PHQ-4, GAD-7 and WHO-5. At the end we ask how you'd like to be contacted and when. Every answer stays in your browser until you press submit.

Start it now
02

Care coordinator review

Within 24–48 hours

A trained care coordinator, a person, not an automated system, reads your answers and works through the verified therapist pool in your country. They weigh language, gender preference, therapy style, availability and the specific difficulty you described. If your answers suggest you need more than online care, or need it faster than we can provide, they tell you that honestly and point you at the right service instead.

03

Direct outreach

On your chosen channel

We contact you on WhatsApp, Telegram, by phone or by email, whichever you picked, at the time of day you said suits you. You get a short introduction to your proposed therapist: who they are, where they trained, how they work, and why the coordinator thinks they fit. If something feels wrong, say so and you'll be offered someone else.

04

Payment & first session

Usually the same working day

Pay through the channel you already use, Telebirr or bank transfer in Ethiopia, M-Pesa in Kenya, MTN or Airtel Money in Rwanda. Send back a screenshot or transaction ID, we verify it by hand, and your session is confirmed in writing with the time and how to join.

In the room

What a session is actually like

Most people have never done this before. That is completely normal.

You lead

There is no exam and no correct way to start. "I don't know where to begin" is a perfectly good first sentence. Your therapist will help you find the thread.

Sessions happen off-platform

Most sessions run over the channel that works best for you: a call, a video link, or messaging. Lebona's code of conduct applies to your therapist there exactly as it would in a clinic.

You can say things in your own language

Switch between Amharic, Kiswahili, Kinyarwanda and English mid-sentence if that is how you think. Your therapist will follow.

Silence is allowed

You are not being billed for performance. Sitting quietly for a minute is often where the useful part starts.

You set the pace on hard things

Trauma work in particular is never rushed. Nothing gets opened before you have somewhere safe to put it down.

Ending is a decision, not a failure

Many people need six to twelve sessions, not years. When you're ready to stop, you and your therapist plan the ending together.

What it costs

Free to start, and priced so money is not why you stop

The self-guided side is permanently free. Sessions are paid locally, per session or in blocks, and nothing is charged before you have been matched.

Privacy

Who can see what

Your intake form

Stored in your own browser while you answer it. On submit it is compiled into our internal care-coordination tracker, where it is read by one coordinator and the therapist you are matched with. We say this plainly because matching is manual: a person really does read it.

Your sessions

Held off-platform on the channel you chose. Your therapist keeps brief clinical notes, as they are professionally required to, and remains bound by Lebona's code of conduct and by their national board.

The one exception

If there is a serious and immediate risk to your life or someone else's, your therapist is obliged to act to keep people safe. They will tell you they are doing it, and involve you wherever possible.
