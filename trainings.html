Track 01 · For individuals

Individual resilience & preventive self-care

Four modules that together make up a practical daily toolkit. Start with the free five-minute version of any of them.

Stress immunity & somatic grounding

Short physical grounding practices, box breathing, progressive muscle relaxation. That regulate the nervous system during ordinary daily tension, not just in a crisis.

Burnout & work-life integration

Identifying early emotional fatigue signs, setting firm personal boundaries, and establishing restorative sleep and screen routines that survive a demanding week.

Mindset & emotional regulation

Cognitive restructuring, "Catch it, Check it, Change it", for managing negative self-talk and intrusive thoughts before they set the tone for the whole day.

First-time therapy prep

A practical primer on how online therapy works, what to expect in a first session, and how to think about the cultural stigma that keeps many people from starting.

Free download

The 7-Day Stress Resilience Toolkit

One exercise a day for a week, as a printable PDF workbook. No account needed. It is the fastest way to find out whether this approach works for you.

04

Refugee & displaced populations

Trauma-informed care & resettlement transition

Recognising triggers from acute displacement, managing survival-mode anxiety, and processing grief, loss and separation from community.

Psychosocial First Aid for frontline responders

Training camp coordinators, intake workers and volunteers to provide initial non-clinical emotional triage during new intake flows.

Culturally adapted coping & narrative therapy

Traditional storytelling, peer circles and collective healing practices that align with the backgrounds people actually come from.

05

At-risk & disadvantaged youth

Youth economic stress & workforce resilience

Managing economic anxiety and unemployment fatigue, and building self-efficacy for young people entering difficult labour markets.

School & community peer mentorship

Training youth leaders to host low-barrier mental health circles, recognise distress in peers, and direct them to a care coordinator.

Digital literacy & mental well-being

Social media pressure, cyberbullying and digital isolation among urban youth across East Africa, and what actually helps.

Protecting aid workers, health staff and social workers from compassion fatigue through structured self-care plans and institutional support protocols.

GBV & complex trauma first response

Peacebuilding & inter-group de-escalation

Step two

Format, duration, languages, cost and, importantly, how we will measure whether it worked. Usually within a week.

Step three

Delivery & follow-up

Training runs, workbooks are distributed, and a coordinator stays on as your point of contact for staff who want individual support.
