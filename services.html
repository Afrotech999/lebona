The intake form asks which of these you want, and routes you accordingly. You can move between them at any time.

How we get the match right

Language, not translation

You are matched with someone who works natively in your language. Feeling is hard enough to describe in your first language.

Verified against national boards

Kenya Counsellors & Psychologists Board, Ethiopian Ministry of Health, Rwanda Allied Health Professions Council, checked before onboarding, re-checked annually.

Style, not just specialism

Gentle or direct, flexible or structured. Getting this wrong is the most common reason people quit therapy in the first month.

Free to change

If the fit is wrong, one message to your coordinator gets you another introduction. No fee, no explanation, no awkwardness.
