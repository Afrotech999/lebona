How we verify credentials

Provider onboarding is standardised across the region. Every therapist is checked directly with the issuing body before they appear here, and re-checked annually.

Ethiopia

Ethiopian Ministry of Health

Professional registration and licence to practise

Kenya

Counsellors & Psychologists Board

Registration under the Counsellors and Psychologists Act

Rwanda

Allied Health Professions Council

Clinical psychology registration and annual licence

We also verify qualifications with the awarding institution, take up references, and require ongoing clinical supervision. Therapists with unresolved complaints are removed from the roster and reported to their board.

Community & cultural resources

Beyond clinicians, we keep contacts for community organisations, women's associations, student services, and faith and traditional leaders who have completed our referral training. Ask a coordinator for the list nearest you. It is kept off the public site so that no one's participation becomes a matter of record.
